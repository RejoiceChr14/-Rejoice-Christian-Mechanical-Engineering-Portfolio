Department pricing initiative.

Reviewing completed departmental projects and organizing information into structured surveys to support a pricing initiative.

My role
Engineering intern
Scope
Completed department projects
Deliverable
Structured project surveys

The department’s goal

Alongside my laboratory documentation work at UL Solutions, I contributed to a pricing initiative that reviewed completed projects across a department. The purpose was to give the company a clearer understanding of the patterns in the work moving through the department.

My contribution

I looked through completed projects and filled out structured surveys containing project information. My work focused on gathering and organizing the information the team needed for its broader review.

The deliverable

The survey responses provided a consistent way to collect project details for trend analysis. My contribution supported the information-gathering stage of the initiative.

What I learned

The project gave me experience reviewing records carefully and organizing information so that it could be compared across different projects. It also helped me see how engineering work connects to the business decisions made around it.

Internal project records, survey responses, pricing, and company data are not shared.
