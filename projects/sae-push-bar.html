UIC Formula SAE · Team concept design

Formula SAE push bar.

Working with teammates on push-bar concepts to move the Formula SAE car without pushing directly on its frame.

My role
Team design contributor
Tool
SolidWorks
Stage
Concept development

The problem

Moving the Formula SAE car by pushing on its frame prompted the team to explore a dedicated push bar. The aim was to give the team a better way to apply the force needed to move the car.

My contribution

I worked with teammates to create and compare different push-bar concepts in SolidWorks. My involvement centered on concept development and communicating design ideas within the team.

Engineering considerations

The project connected CAD work to a practical mechanical question: how to introduce a movement force into the vehicle through an appropriate interface. A useful concept also has to account for the people moving the car and the space around its existing components.

Project stage

My contribution reached the design-concept stage. The work described here is the team’s exploration of alternative layouts, rather than a completed fabrication or validation program.

What I learned and would develop next

The experience gave me practice presenting CAD ideas, considering alternatives, and working toward a shared mechanical design goal. Further development would require confirming the attachment arrangement, checking clearances and loads, and evaluating a prototype before use.
